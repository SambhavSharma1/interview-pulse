## Slide 1: The Problem Statement
### "The Fresh Graduate Interview Trap"
* **The Reality:** Over 80% of Computer Science and university graduates know theoretical syntax, but freeze, stumble, or get rejected during technical and viva interviews.
* **Why It Happens:**
  1. **Interview Anxiety & Isolation:** Practicing on LeetCode or watching YouTube is passive; it fails to prepare candidates for verbal pressure under viva boards or corporate panels.
  2. **Prohibitive Coaching Costs:** 1-on-1 human mock interviews cost \$40 to \$100/hour—completely out of reach for students in South Asia.
  3. **Zero Diagnostic Feedback:** Generic rejection emails ("We moved forward with other candidates") never explain *why* the candidate failed (e.g., missing quantifiable results, speaking too fast, or overusing filler words).

---

## Slide 2: The Proposed Solution
### "InterviewPulse: 24/7 AI Mock Interviewer & Readiness Coach"
* **What It Is:** An accessible, browser-native AI career coach that simulates realistic multi-turn technical, viva, and embassy interviews.
* **High-Impact Differentiators:**
  * **Resume + Job Tailoring:** Extracts custom questions directly from the candidate's CV and target job description.
  * **Local-Context Modes:** Beyond tech, supports **Bank & Govt Job Viva (BCS/Banking)** and **Student Visa & Scholarship Interviews (US F1, UK, Chevening)**.
  * **Speech & Pace Analytics:** Calculates Words Per Minute (WPM) and detects filler words ("um", "like", "actually") in real time.
  * **STAR-Method Scoring:** Automatically checks for Situation, Task, Action, and Result to guide candidates toward quantifiable answers.
  * **Interactive "Try Again" Loop:** Compare side-by-side with an improved model answer and re-answer to watch scores rise.
  * **Anxiety-Aware Modes & Pre-Interview Breathing:** Includes a built-in 8-second box breathing protocol.

---

## Slide 3: How the Solution Works
### "Architecture & Technical Workflow"
* **Step 1: Context & Resume Upload**
  * Candidate selects their track (Tech Fresher, Corporate HR, Govt Viva, Visa Interview, Freelancer) and pastes CV/Job bullets.
  * Selects pressure mode (Gentle, Realistic, Stress Viva) and takes an optional 8-second box-breathing warmup.
* **Step 2: Real-Time Dynamic Simulation**
  * The AI Interviewer speaks questions aloud using voice synthesis (TTS).
  * Candidate answers verbally via Web Speech API or by typing, while the engine tracks WPM and hesitation markers.
* **Step 3: Multi-Axis Diagnostic Engine**
  * Generates an interactive **5-Axis Competency Radar** (Content, Structure, Confidence, Clarity, Relevance).
  * Highlights green strengths, red flags ("Missing Result", "Filler word overuse"), and ideal senior answers.
* **Step 4: Practice History & Shareable PDF**
  * Local storage tracks improvement streaks, and one-click PDF export lets candidates share their 7-day recovery roadmap.
